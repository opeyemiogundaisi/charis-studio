The difference

Follows the service, not the other way round.

Charis Studio hears what’s said and sung and lines up what comes next. You choose whether it asks first or puts it straight on screen after a short countdown you can cancel.

Speech recognition runs on your PC, so it keeps working when the internet doesn’t. Online speech and AI matching are there when you want extra accuracy.

Questions

Before you install.

Does it need the internet?

No. Charis Studio, your licence and the listening features all work offline. You need the internet to stream, and for updates.

What PC do I need?

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. For streaming and recording, a graphics card (NVIDIA, Intel or AMD) gives the smoothest results.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”.

That appears for newer publishers. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”. Charis Studio then installs and opens by itself.

How do updates work?

Charis Studio downloads updates in the background and applies them the next time you open it, never in the middle of a service.

Can I use my phone as a camera?

Yes. Install the free DroidCam app on the phone, join the same Wi-Fi, and add it in Charis Studio. Nothing extra to install on the PC.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. Charis Studio is for Windows today; a Mac version is planned.
